Please note

We no longer answer support questions on social media

That includes comments and messages on Skool, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, Substack and LinkedIn.

Here’s why: when order changes, requests to add products and order-status questions arrive across several platforms, the details can get scattered. A comment on one platform and a message on another make it hard to follow the full conversation, and we don’t want anyone’s request to get missed. Keeping support in one place helps us stay organized, follow your requests accurately and take better care of you.

Still waiting on an answer from social media? Please send it to support@glowglps.com or call (844) 456-9522.

Thank you for helping us keep customer care running smoothly. — Heather & the Glow Lab Protocols Team
