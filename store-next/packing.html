Shows every order marked Paid, plus orders whose label is printed but UPS has not scanned yet (last 7 days), oldest first. Press the space bar once for every vial as it goes in the bag (or tap the boxes). The gold box shows what to put in next. Ticks, skips and "out of stock" marks stay on this device. When a box is fully checked it is marked Packed in the store too, so every computer and Management see it (Start over un-marks it). 🧾 Slip prints a 4×6 packing slip with a tracking QR on the label printer. If an order is edited after you checked it, its ticks clear so you check it again. Orders refresh by themselves every minute while you are not typing — every new order pops up in the corner with a cha-ching (and on your computer, if order pop-ups are on).
