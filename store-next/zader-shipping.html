How shipping works

  • We pack and ship your order after your payment has been checked.
  • Orders ship by UPS, tracked, in plain packaging with nothing on the outside that says what is inside.
  • You get an email with your tracking number as soon as your shipping label is created.
  • Delivery dates come from UPS and are estimates, not guarantees.

Tracking hasn't moved?

A label that shows as created but not yet scanned usually means the package hasn't been scanned in at UPS yet. Give it one business day. If it still hasn't moved, email jonahglowwithheather@gmail.com with your order number and we will chase UPS for you.

Changing or canceling an order

If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact us right away with your order number and we will do our best to change or cancel it. Once the package is with UPS, we can't call it back.

Returns

Because these are research materials, we can't accept returns or give refunds once an order has shipped, except where the law requires it.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

If anything arrives damaged, wrong or missing, email jonahglowwithheather@gmail.com or call or text 943-241-8176 as soon as you can. Please include your order number and a photo of what you received. We will replace the item or refund it.
