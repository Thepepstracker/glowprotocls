Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This page explains what information Zader Health collects when you use zaderhealth.com, why we collect it, and who we share it with. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

What we collect

  • Your order details: your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, the products you order, and the payment method you choose.
  • Your payment screenshot: when you pay, you upload a screenshot of the payment so we can match it to your order. We do not see or store your card or bank account numbers.
  • Messages you send us by email, phone or text.
  • Basic technical information that any website receives, such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting provider keeps in its server logs.

How we use it

  • To check your payment, pack and ship your order, and send you order and shipping emails.
  • To answer your questions and sort out any problem with an order.
  • To keep the records the law requires us to keep, and to prevent fraud or misuse.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the store, and only what each one needs:

  • Netlify, which hosts the website and stores order records.
  • Resend, which sends our order and shipping emails.
  • ShipStation and UPS, which receive your name, address and phone number to make the shipping label and deliver your package.
  • The payment service you choose (Venmo, PayPal, Cash App or Zelle). You pay them directly, under their own privacy policies.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the lettering on this site, so your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

Cookies and storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser keeps your bag and order details in its own storage while you shop, and a sign-in cookie is used only if you sign in.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them to run the business and meet tax and legal requirements, then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Email jonahglowwithheather@gmail.com and we will reply within a few business days. We may need to keep some order records where the law requires it.

Children

This site is for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
