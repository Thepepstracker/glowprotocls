Common questions

What does it cost to join?

Nothing. Create an account and you are in. There is no fee and no minimum order.

Exactly how many points do I earn?

10 points for every $10 of order value. A $50 order earns 50 points, a $120 order earns 120 points. Points are awarded in $10 steps, so a $59 order earns the same as a $50 order.

What are my points worth?

100 points takes $5 off your order, so points are worth about 5% back on what you spend.

When do points show up?

After your order is marked complete. If you do not see them right away, give it until your order ships.

Do points expire?

No. Your balance stays put until you use it.

Can I use points and a member discount together?

Yes. Your Glow+ or Glow Elite discount applies automatically alongside your points. Promotional codes may be limited to one per order.

What happens if I return something?

Points earned on a refunded order are reversed. If you already redeemed them, we will sort it out with you directly.

Is this the same as the affiliate program?

No. Glow Loyalty rewards you for your own orders. The Affiliate Program pays you for orders you refer to us. You can be in both.
