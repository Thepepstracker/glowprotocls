How it works

  1. Create your affiliate account

    Use the Create Your Affiliate Account button to register with our GoAffPro portal. Follow any verification or approval instructions before promoting.

  2. Set up your dashboard

    In Settings, complete your profile and available payment details. In Home, copy your personal customer referral link and assigned discount code. Share those exact details, rather than the generic signup page, with shoppers.

  3. Share responsibly

    Use approved resources in Marketing Tools when available. Clearly disclose that you may earn a commission. Research materials must not be promoted for human or animal use.

  4. Invite other affiliates

    To recruit affiliates, use your personal signup link in the Network tab, when available. This is different from your customer shopping link. If Network is missing, contact support before recruiting so your referral can be linked correctly for the 5% override.

  5. Review earnings and payments

    Review attributed orders and commissions in Home, and payment history in Payments. Check the program terms for approval, payout timing and eligibility. A recorded referral is not a guarantee of payment.
