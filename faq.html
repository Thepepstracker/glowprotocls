Storage & Handling

Most lyophilized peptides are stored at −20 °C, and at 2–8 °C once in solution. Storage is not uniform across the catalogue, though — Lipo-C with B-12 is refrigerated at 2–8 °C, GHK-Cu and Glutathione should additionally be protected from light and oxidation, and the laboratory reagents are stored at room temperature.

The exact storage condition for every product is stated in the Specifications table on its product page. Our full Storage & Handling guide covers the detail.

Most lyophilized peptides are reconstituted with bacteriostatic water (sterile water with 0.9% benzyl alcohol). Where a compound does not fully dissolve in neutral aqueous media, researchers use 0.6% acetic acid as the diluent instead.

Both are in the catalogue as laboratory reagents. Volumes depend on the concentration your protocol calls for — see the Storage & Handling guide.

Lyophilized powder is considerably more stable in transit than reconstituted solution, and brief time at ambient temperature during shipping is normal and expected.

If a vial arrives visibly damaged, cracked, discoloured or already in solution, contact courtney@glowwithheather.com promptly — damaged or incorrect items are the exception to our returns policy.

Some compounds are genuinely coloured — GHK-Cu, for instance, is a blue lyophilized powder because of its copper complex. Beyond that, the physical appearance of a lyophilized cake can vary between batches without indicating a problem.

If something looks wrong to you, email us with a photo before using it and we will look at the batch documentation with you.
