What a certificate does and does not prove

The honest version

Testing is performed by Horizon Analytical, an independent laboratory, on the compound and batch supplied to us. We do not run the testing ourselves, and we do not pay for a favourable result.

A displayed certificate may not correspond to the exact vial you receive. Certificates come from our supplier and cover the batch we sourced. Batch and lot numbers change over time. We make reasonable efforts to show the certificate matching what we are shipping, but we will not claim more than that.

If you need a certificate for a specific batch, ask before you order rather than after. We would rather answer the question than take the order.

Certificates remain the property of the issuing laboratory and our supplier, and are linked here for reference. Full terms are in our Terms & Conditions.

Read a certificate before you buy anything

It is the fairest test of a peptide company, and the one we would want applied to us.
